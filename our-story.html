Our story

An old shop on North Street, with a future again

Owner photo

Jonathan

Owner, Laundry Lounge

The idea for Laundry Lounge came after I restored an old shop on North Street in Bo'ness. I was proud to see the building brought back to life, but I still had an important decision to make. Whatever opened behind that door had to serve a real purpose for the people who lived nearby.

What drew me to a laundrette was how much difference it could make to an ordinary week. For a family, it might mean getting several loads washed in one visit and having the afternoon together. For someone without a tumble dryer, it might mean taking dry clothes home on a wet winter evening. I wanted people in Bo'ness to have somewhere close to home they could turn to for those everyday things.

"Whatever opened behind that door had to serve a real purpose for the people who lived nearby."

That thinking shaped the shop itself. I chose larger machines so people could bring the bedding they struggle to wash at home. I made it self-service and kept the doors open from early morning until late evening so a visit could fit around work or the school run. And I wanted it to feel welcoming enough that anyone coming in for the first time could put a wash on, sit down with a coffee and feel at ease.

My hope is that Laundry Lounge becomes a familiar part of life in Bo'ness. It means a great deal to me that an old shop on North Street has a future again, and that its future is tied to the people who use it.

Jonathan
