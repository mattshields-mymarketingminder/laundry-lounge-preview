Your first visit

Never used a laundrette? It takes about a minute to get going.

Here's exactly what happens when you walk in, so your first visit feels familiar.

STEP 1

Load a machine

Pick a washer that suits your load: 9kg, 14kg or 18kg. No need to bring detergent or softener, they're added automatically.

STEP 2

Choose and pay

Select your machine number on the tablet beside the entrance, or on your own phone, and pay by contactless. Your wash starts.

STEP 3

Relax, then dry

Grab a coffee and take a seat. When it's done, move it to a dryer, from £1.50 per five minutes, and head home with it all done.
